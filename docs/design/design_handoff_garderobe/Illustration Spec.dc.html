export
PNG at 3× for raster, SVG preferred where the artwork is flat. Transparent background unless the slot names a ground colour.
palette to work in
Oxblood, blush, butter, ink, cream, sage. No gradients, no drop shadows.
house style
Flat, line-led, editorial. Figures cropped or faceless. Garments drawn accurately; props minimal. Ground can bleed to the slot edge.
