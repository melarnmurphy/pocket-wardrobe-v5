Four rules that hold across all six frames
·Nothing is written until frame 06. Every earlier step edits local state, so cancel is free at any point.
·Frames 02, 04 and 05 are one component. If you find yourself writing a third variant, the difference belongs in the candidate's slot, not in a new screen.
·The zoom is a transform on a persistent element, not a screen transition. The user must see garment 1 leave the group it came from.
·Dropping a garment is always one tap away, and dropping all of them lands on "keep whole" rather than an error.
